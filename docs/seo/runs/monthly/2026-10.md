## 5 key facts

1. ~200 pages built for the AI/tutor strategy (45 AI-alternative pages, 51 profession triplets, 13 what-to-teach-next, 95 pSEO) produced **15 clicks in 3 months**.
2. 5 free tools + 7 feature pages (12 URLs) produced **42 clicks** — the best-yielding surface on the site; `/tools/vocab-cefr-checker` is the #1 page.
3. **38 URLs that earned 34 clicks and 1,042 impressions now return 404**; that is 11% of all clicks lost without a redirect.
4. Indexed pages are eroding (−10% since July) and crawled-not-indexed rose 27 → 128: Google is rejecting pages after crawling them.
5. Zero search demand reaches the target-ICP terms (tutor, private, adult, Business English, ChatGPT alternative); actual Google traffic is general ESL teachers looking for activities, CEFR tools and worksheets.
