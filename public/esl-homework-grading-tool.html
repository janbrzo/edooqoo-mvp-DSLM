ESL Homework Review Tool with AI Assistance

Edooqoo provides an AI-assisted homework review workflow for ESL/EFL teachers. Students submit exercises online, the system prepares suggested evaluation signals, and the teacher reviews answers, adjusts scores, adds comments, and confirms the final result.

This page describes the public homework-review mechanics. It does not claim that AI replaces teacher judgment or that every answer can be evaluated without human review.

Problem

English homework often includes open-ended answers, grammar transformations, paraphrases, reading responses, and short written explanations. These tasks require more than exact-match checking because multiple correct answers may exist.

Edooqoo.com Solution

Edooqoo connects homework assignment, student submission, AI-assisted answer review, teacher confirmation, and student progress tracking in one workflow. The teacher remains responsible for final scoring and feedback.

Technical Mechanics

  1. Create or select a worksheet - choose a worksheet with exercises suitable for independent work.
  2. Assign homework - select exercises, set a deadline, and send the student a homework link.
  3. Student submits online - answers are stored with the homework submission.
  4. AI-assisted review - the system prepares suggested scores or evaluation notes where supported.
  5. Teacher confirms - the teacher reviews the submission, edits scores if needed, adds comments, and confirms the outcome.
  6. Progress updates - confirmed results can feed student progress surfaces.

Teacher Review Workflow

  1. Open the submitted homework.
  2. Review suggested scores and student answers side by side.
  3. Adjust scores where the suggested evaluation misses nuance.
  4. Add comments or corrections for the student.
  5. Confirm the reviewed homework.

FAQ

Does AI replace teacher review?

No. Edooqoo positions homework evaluation as teacher-reviewed AI assistance. The teacher confirms the final score and feedback.

Do students need to install anything?

No. Students complete homework in a browser through a shared link or Student Hub access.

Can teachers add their own comments?

Yes. Teachers can add comments and corrections during review.

Can homework results affect progress tracking?

Confirmed homework results can contribute to student progress surfaces inside the product.
