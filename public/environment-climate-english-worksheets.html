Best Exercise Types

1. Reading Comprehension

Articles about climate change, renewable energy initiatives, or conservation projects. Students read and answer questions that test both comprehension and vocabulary acquisition.

2. Discussion Questions

Debates and opinion exchange: "Should plastic bags be banned?", "Is nuclear energy the solution?", "What can individuals do about climate change?" Perfect for B2-C1 speaking practice.

3. Vocabulary Exercises

Fill in the blanks with environment vocabulary, match terms with definitions, categorize words by topic. Essential for building the specialized vocabulary needed for this topic.

4. Sentence Transformation

Practice passive voice (environmental processes), conditionals (solutions and consequences), and reported speech (quoting scientists and politicians) — all naturally connect to environment topics.

Grammar Through Environment Topics

  • Passive Voice: "Forests are being destroyed." "The ozone layer was damaged by CFCs."
  • Conditionals: "If we don't reduce emissions, temperatures will rise." "If everyone recycled, we would reduce waste."
  • Comparatives: "Solar energy is cleaner than coal." "Wind power is becoming more affordable."
  • Future forms: "Sea levels are going to rise." "By 2050, renewable energy will dominate."
