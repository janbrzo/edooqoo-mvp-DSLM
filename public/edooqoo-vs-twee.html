Edooqoo vs Twee — AI Worksheet Generators Compared

Both Edooqoo and Twee use AI to generate English teaching materials, but they serve different needs. Twee focuses on AI content generation — creating texts, dialogues, and exercises. Edooqoo provides a complete teaching ecosystem: AI worksheet generation with 29 exercise types, plus homework with AI grading, smart flashcards, student management, progress tracking, lesson calendar, and a Student Hub portal.

If you only need to generate occasional texts or exercises, Twee can be helpful. But if you want a platform that manages your entire teaching workflow — from placing students at the right level to tracking their progress over months — Edooqoo is the more comprehensive solution. Every worksheet is tied to a student profile and feeds into long-term progress data.

Edooqoo's key advantage is its ecosystem approach: worksheets aren't standalone documents but part of a connected system. Exercise results update nano-skill mastery metrics, vocabulary feeds into flashcard sets, and AI uses all this data to generate increasingly personalized materials for each student.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Twee free for English teachers?

Twee offers a free tier with limited generations. Edooqoo provides 2 free AI-generated worksheets on signup, then plans from $9/mo with 15 worksheets/month and all ecosystem features included.

Does Twee track student progress?

No. Twee is focused on content generation without student management. Edooqoo tracks each student's skills at the nano-skill level, shows mastery trends, and provides AI suggestions for future lessons.

Can Twee assign homework?

Twee doesn't have a homework system. Edooqoo lets you assign worksheets as homework with deadlines, email notifications, AI grading of open-ended answers, and teacher review tools.

Which has more exercise types?

Edooqoo offers 29 specialized exercise types organized in 3 categories: 20 basic (reading comprehension, fill in the blanks, error correction, etc.), 5 audio (listening comprehension, audio fill in the blanks, etc.), and 4 picture (describe picture, picture-based questions).

Can I manage students in Twee?

Twee doesn't offer student management. Edooqoo lets you add unlimited students, track their progress, manage flashcards, assign homework, schedule lessons, and give them access to a dedicated Student Hub portal.
