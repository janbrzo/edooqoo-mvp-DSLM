Try It: Speaking Task for Adult Learners (B1)

A short task in the style Edooqoo produces, set in a real adult context: small talk and meeting contributions. Use it as is in your next lesson, or generate an editable version tuned to your learner.

  1. Give a polite interruption phrase for a meeting.
  2. Respond naturally: "How was your weekend?"
  3. Give a phrase to buy time before answering.
Answer key

1. Sorry to interrupt, could I add something here?
2. Pretty good, thanks. We went hiking. How about yours?
3. That's a good question. Let me think for a second.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Edooqoo for group online classes?

Yes. Live Session mode lets you see all students' answers in real-time, add exercises on-the-fly, and manage up to 12 exercises per session. Multiple students can work on the same worksheet simultaneously.

Does Edooqoo work with Zoom or Google Meet?

Edooqoo integrates directly with Google Meet; it can auto-generate Meet links for booked lessons. For Zoom, you can add your Zoom link to lesson notes. Edooqoo handles the teaching materials while you use any video platform.

How do students access materials?

Students access everything through the Student Hub portal or via direct links. They don't need to create accounts or install software, everything works in the browser.

Is Edooqoo suitable for freelance online teachers?

Absolutely. Edooqoo is designed for independent teachers with features like lesson booking, payment tracking, and a public booking page that students can use to schedule lessons.

What devices do students need?

Any device with a web browser: laptop, tablet, or smartphone. All exercises, flashcards, homework, and the Student Hub work on any modern browser without installation.
