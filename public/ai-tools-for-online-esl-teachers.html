Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Edooqoo during a Zoom/Skype lesson?

Yes! Share the worksheet link in the chat or share your screen. Use Live Session mode to see student answers in real-time. The Draw on Worksheet feature lets you annotate during the lesson — highlight text, circle answers, and draw diagrams.

How does lesson booking handle time zones?

The calendar system handles time zone conversion automatically. You set your availability in your timezone, and students see available slots in their timezone. Google Calendar events are created with correct times for both parties.

Can I generate Google Meet links automatically?

Yes. When enabled in Calendar Settings, Edooqoo automatically generates a Google Meet link for each booked lesson and adds it to the calendar event.

What happens between online lessons?

Assign homework with specific exercises and deadlines. Students complete them via the direct link or Student Hub. They can also study flashcards with spaced repetition. You see completion status and AI-graded results before the next session.

Can students from different countries use the Student Hub?

Yes. The Student Hub works in any browser, anywhere in the world. Students enter their email, select their teacher, and access all their materials — worksheets, flashcards, homework, and lesson booking — in one place.
