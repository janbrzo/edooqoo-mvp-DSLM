Teaching English One-to-One — Private Lesson Guide

March 2026 · Specialized

One-to-one teaching offers the ultimate in personalization, but it also requires unique skills. Without the energy of group dynamics, private lessons demand careful planning, strong rapport, and adaptive teaching. This guide covers everything you need to deliver excellent private English lessons.

Benefits of 1-on-1 Teaching

  • 100% personalized content and pacing
  • Maximum speaking time for the student
  • Immediate error correction and feedback
  • Flexible scheduling and lesson structure
  • Deep understanding of student needs

First Lesson Framework

Needs Analysis (15-20 min)

Ask about goals, timeline, preferred learning style, previous experience, and specific situations where they need English. Use Edooqoo's AI placement test for objective level assessment.

Level Assessment (15-20 min)

Through natural conversation, assess speaking, listening, grammar range, vocabulary, and pronunciation. Note specific areas for development.

Lesson Plan Discussion (10 min)

Share your proposed approach, frequency, homework expectations, and tools you'll use. Set clear expectations from day one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep 1-on-1 lessons from becoming boring?

Vary activity types every 10-15 minutes. Use multimedia, games, role-plays, and real-world tasks. Follow the student's interests. Alternate between skills (speaking, reading, writing, listening). Make every lesson feel different.

How much should I charge for private lessons?

Pricing depends on your location, experience, qualifications, and market. Research local rates. Generally, online lessons range from $15-50/hour; in-person from $25-80/hour. Offer package discounts for commitment.
