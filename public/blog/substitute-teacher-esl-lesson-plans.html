Substitute Teacher ESL Lesson Plans — Ready to Go

March 2026 · Lesson Resources

Whether you're covering for a colleague or facing an unexpected teaching situation, having ready-to-go lesson plans is essential. These activities work at any level, require minimal preparation, and keep students engaged without prior knowledge of the class.

5 Universal Emergency Lesson Plans

1. The Interview Lesson (All Levels, 60 min)

Students prepare 5-8 questions on a topic (hobbies, travel, work). They interview a partner, take notes, and present their partner to the class. Practices question formation, listening, note-taking, and presentation.

2. Picture Description (All Levels, 45-60 min)

Show an interesting image. Students describe it, speculate about context, create a story, or write a dialogue. Different tasks for different levels.

3. Word Association Web (A2+, 30-45 min)

Start with one word on the board. Students add connected words, building a vocabulary web. Discuss connections, practice spelling, and use words in sentences.

4. Story Chain (B1+, 30-45 min)

First student says one sentence to start a story. Each student adds one sentence. Write the story on the board. Edit together for grammar.

5. Speed Debates (B1+, 30-45 min)

Write 5 controversial statements. Pairs discuss for 3 minutes each, then switch partners. Students practice giving opinions and using linking words.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I don't know the students' level?

Start with a simple activity and observe. Ask students to introduce themselves — this quickly reveals their level. Have tiered versions ready: simplify for beginners, extend for advanced learners.
