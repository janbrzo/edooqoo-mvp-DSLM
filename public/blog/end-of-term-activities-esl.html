End-of-Term Activities for ESL Classes

March 2026 · Lesson Resources

The last class deserves as much thought as the first. End-of-term activities should celebrate progress, review key learning, provide closure, and leave students with positive memories and motivation to continue learning.

Review Activities

  • Quiz show: Team-based review game covering the term's topics
  • Vocabulary bingo: Using all vocabulary from the course
  • Grammar auction: Students bid on whether sentences are correct
  • Timeline of learning: Students create a visual timeline of what they learned

Reflection Activities

  • Progress letter: Students write a letter to their future selves about what they've learned
  • Before and after: Compare first-day writing samples with current work
  • Three things: Share three things learned, three favorite activities, three goals

Celebration Ideas

  • Class photo and shared memories
  • Certificate of completion
  • Student presentations on their progress
  • Potluck with food from different cultures

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I maintain learning on the last day?

Even celebration activities can be learning opportunities. A quiz game reviews content. Reflection writing practices writing skills. Presentations develop speaking. Frame fun activities with clear language objectives.
