Using Google Workspace for ESL Teachers

March 2026 · Technology

Google Workspace offers a powerful, free toolkit for ESL teachers. From creating collaborative worksheets in Google Docs to building quizzes in Google Forms and managing classes in Google Classroom, these tools can streamline your teaching workflow significantly.

Google Docs for ESL Teaching

Collaborative Writing

Use shared documents for peer writing activities. Students can write essays, provide feedback using the commenting feature, and track revisions. The suggestion mode lets teachers provide feedback without overwriting student work.

Worksheet Templates

Create reusable worksheet templates with formatted tables, text boxes, and instructions. However, manual worksheet creation is time-consuming. AI tools like Edooqoo generate complete worksheets in under 2 minutes, saving hours of formatting time.

Google Forms for Assessment

  • Quick quizzes: Auto-graded multiple choice and short answer
  • Placement tests: Section branching based on answers
  • Student feedback: Anonymous course evaluation surveys
  • Needs analysis: Questionnaires for new students

Google Slides for Presentations

Create visual lesson presentations with embedded videos, images, and interactive elements. Use the Q&A feature for student questions during online lessons. Share slides as study resources after class.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Google Workspace free for teachers?

Yes, Google Workspace for Education is free for schools. Individual teachers can use personal Google accounts with full access to Docs, Slides, Forms, and a limited version of Classroom.

Can I integrate Google Calendar with lesson scheduling?

Yes. Tools like Edooqoo offer direct Google Calendar sync, creating events automatically when lessons are booked. You can also generate Google Meet links for online lessons.

How do I organize materials for multiple students?

Create a shared Google Drive folder for each student or class. Use consistent naming conventions (e.g., "StudentName_Level_Date"). For private tutors managing many students, a dedicated platform like Edooqoo with its student management system is more efficient.
