The Human + AI Sweet Spot

AI excels at: generating content, grading objective items, tracking data, automating scheduling. Humans excel at: building relationships, motivating students, adapting in real-time, providing emotional support, making pedagogical decisions. The best teachers in 2026 use AI for the former so they can focus on the latter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will AI replace ESL teachers?

No. AI can generate materials and provide practice, but it cannot build relationships, read a student's emotional state, adapt a lesson in real-time based on subtle cues, or provide the human connection that motivates learning. AI is a tool that makes good teachers better — it doesn't replace them.

How do I start using AI if I'm not tech-savvy?

Start with ONE tool and one use case. Try Edooqoo for worksheet creation — it's designed for teachers, not technologists. Generate one worksheet, see the quality, and build from there. You don't need to adopt everything at once.
