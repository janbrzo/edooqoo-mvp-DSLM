Using Podcasts in ESL Teaching — Activities and Lesson Plans

March 2026 · Listening

Podcasts are a goldmine for ESL teachers: free, abundant, authentic, and covering every topic imaginable. But simply telling students to "listen to podcasts" isn't teaching — you need structured activities that turn passive listening into active learning.

Choosing the Right Podcast

LevelTypeRecommendations
A2-B1ESL-specific6 Minute English (BBC), ESL Pod, English Learning for Curious Minds
B1-B2Simplified authenticTED Talks Daily, Podcasts in English, Luke's English Podcast
B2-C2AuthenticThis American Life, Freakonomics, BBC World Service

Lesson Framework

  1. Pre-listening (5 min) — Introduce topic, teach key vocabulary, set prediction questions
  2. First listen (5-10 min) — Listen for gist: "What is the main topic?"
  3. Second listen (5-10 min) — Listen for detail with specific questions
  4. Post-listening (10 min) — Discussion, vocabulary work, reaction writing

Activity Ideas

  1. Gap-fill transcripts while listening
  2. Note-taking practice with structured templates
  3. Summarize the episode in 3 sentences
  4. Debate the podcast's main argument
  5. Vocabulary hunt — find and define 5 new words

FAQ

How long should podcast segments be?

3-5 minutes for A2-B1, 5-10 minutes for B2+. Use clips, not full episodes. Students can listen to full episodes independently as homework.
