Best Digital Homework Tools for ESL Teachers in 2026

March 2026 · Homework

Digital homework tools save ESL teachers hours of work every week by automating assignment delivery, tracking completion, and providing instant feedback. But with dozens of options available, choosing the right platform matters.

What to Look For

  • ESL-specific exercise types (fill-in-the-blanks, matching, multiple choice)
  • Automatic or AI-assisted grading
  • Student progress tracking and analytics
  • Easy sharing (links, no student accounts needed)
  • Mobile-friendly interface
  • Affordable pricing for independent teachers

Why AI Grading Changes Everything

Traditional auto-grading only works for objective exercises (multiple choice, true/false). AI grading evaluates open-ended responses — writing tasks, gap-fill with multiple correct answers, and even speaking exercises. This means students get instant, detailed feedback on the exercises that matter most.

FAQ

Do students need accounts?

Depends on the platform. Edooqoo uses share links — students access homework via URL without creating accounts. This reduces friction significantly, especially for adult learners.

Can I combine multiple tools?

Yes, and many teachers do. Use Edooqoo for AI-graded exercises, Quizlet for vocabulary review, and Google Docs for writing tasks. Choose tools based on the skill being practiced.
