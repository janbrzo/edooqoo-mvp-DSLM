Cambridge Exam Preparation — Tips and Worksheet Strategies for Teachers

Published March 11, 2026 · Exam & Business

Cambridge English exams are the gold standard of English language certification. With over 5.5 million candidates annually, Cambridge exams (KET, PET, FCE, CAE, CPE) are recognized by 25,000+ organizations worldwide. Preparing students effectively requires deep understanding of exam formats, strategic exercise selection, and extensive practice — which is where AI worksheet generation becomes invaluable.

This guide provides practical strategies for each Cambridge exam level, explains which Edooqoo exercise types best mirror exam formats, and offers study schedule templates to structure your preparation courses.

Exercise Types per Exam

KET (A2 Key)

Focus on: Multiple choice reading, matching, fill in the blanks (simple), short writing tasks. Use Edooqoo at A2 level with Multiple Choice, Matching, Fill in the Blanks, and True/False exercises.

PET (B1 Preliminary)

Focus on: Sentence transformation (basic), reading comprehension, dialogue completion, letter writing. Use Edooqoo at B1 with Sentence Transformation, Reading Comprehension, Dialogue Practice, and Answer Questions.

FCE (B2 First)

Focus on: Use of English (open cloze, word formation, sentence transformation), reading comprehension (multiple matching, gapped text), essay writing, listening (multiple choice). Use Edooqoo at B2 with Sentence Transformation, Gap Text (Cloze), Error Correction, Reading Comprehension, Multiple Choice, and Listening Comprehension.

CAE (C1 Advanced)

Focus on: Advanced Use of English (sentence transformation with 3-5 word gap, word formation, multiple choice cloze), complex reading, proposals/reports. Use Edooqoo at C1 with advanced Sentence Transformation, Paraphrasing, Reading Comprehension, and Discussion Questions.

CPE (C2 Proficiency)

Focus on: Sophisticated language use, nuanced comprehension, academic summary writing. Use Edooqoo at C2 with Paraphrasing, Reading Comprehension, Discussion Questions, and advanced Sentence Transformation.

Common Student Mistakes

  • KET/PET: Poor time management, not reading instructions carefully, guessing without elimination.
  • FCE: Ignoring Use of English preparation, writing off-topic, not using a range of structures.
  • CAE/CPE: Not demonstrating C1/C2 vocabulary range, using only simple sentence structures, ignoring register in writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should exam prep start?

Minimum 8-12 weeks for students at the target level. For students who need to improve their level first, add 4-8 weeks of general improvement before exam-specific preparation.

Can AI replace official practice tests?

No. Use official Cambridge past papers for 2-3 full practice tests under exam conditions. Use Edooqoo for unlimited supplementary practice between official tests.

Which exercise types are most important for FCE?

Use of English exercises (sentence transformation, word formation, open cloze) are unique to FCE and typically the most challenging paper. Prioritize these in your preparation.
