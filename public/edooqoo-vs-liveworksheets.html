Edooqoo vs Liveworksheets — Which is Better for English Teachers?

Liveworksheets lets teachers convert static PDF/image worksheets into interactive online versions. Teachers manually add interactive fields (drag-and-drop, dropdowns, text inputs) to existing materials. Edooqoo eliminates this manual step entirely — AI generates complete interactive worksheets from scratch in under 60 seconds, with 29 specialized exercise types designed specifically for English teaching.

The fundamental difference: Liveworksheets digitizes existing materials, while Edooqoo creates new, personalized materials using AI. With Liveworksheets, you still need to design the worksheet first. With Edooqoo, you describe what you need (topic, level, grammar focus) and AI does the rest — including answer keys, vocabulary sheets, and audio exercises.

Edooqoo also provides a complete teaching ecosystem that Liveworksheets lacks: homework with AI grading, smart flashcards with SM-2 spaced repetition, nano-skill progress tracking, a Student Hub portal, lesson booking with Google Calendar, and a 49-question placement test.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Liveworksheets free?

Liveworksheets offers a free tier with limits on the number of interactive worksheets you can create. Premium plans remove limits. Edooqoo offers 2 free AI-generated worksheets, then plans from $9/mo for 15 worksheets/month with all features included.

Can Liveworksheets generate worksheets with AI?

No. Liveworksheets requires you to upload an existing worksheet and manually add interactive elements. Edooqoo generates complete worksheets from scratch using AI — just specify the topic, level, and exercise types.

Which platform is faster for creating materials?

Edooqoo is significantly faster. AI generates a complete worksheet in under 60 seconds. With Liveworksheets, you first need to create or find a worksheet, then spend 15-30 minutes adding interactive elements manually.

Does Liveworksheets support English-specific exercise types?

Liveworksheets supports generic interactive elements (dropdowns, drag-and-drop, text input). Edooqoo offers 29 English-specific exercise types including Reading Comprehension, Error Correction, Sentence Transformation, Listening Comprehension, and Describe Picture.

Can I track student progress on Liveworksheets?

Liveworksheets has a basic gradebook. Edooqoo tracks skills at the nano-skill level (e.g., "B1.grammar.present_perfect.negative"), shows mastery trends, and provides AI-generated suggestions for future lessons based on skill gaps.
