All 29 Exercise Types in Edooqoo

Edooqoo's AI generates 29 different exercise types across text, audio, and picture formats. Every exercise is calibrated to CEFR levels A1 through C2 and generated in under 2 minutes.

Text-Based Exercises (20)

Reading Comprehension

AI generates an original text on any topic at the target CEFR level, followed by comprehension questions.

A1-C2 Best for: developing reading skills, vocabulary in context

Fill in the Blanks

Sentences with missing words. Students choose from provided options or write answers.

A1-C2 Best for: grammar practice, vocabulary reinforcement

Multiple Choice

Questions with 3-4 answer options. Auto-graded instantly.

A1-C2 Best for: quick assessment, grammar, vocabulary

True/False

Students determine whether statements are correct or incorrect based on a text or general knowledge.

A1-C2 Best for: reading comprehension, factual recall

Matching

Connect items from two columns — words to definitions, synonyms, or translations.

A1-C2 Best for: vocabulary building, concept association

Dialogue Practice

Complete or create conversational exchanges in real-world situations.

A1-B2 Best for: functional language, speaking preparation

Answer Questions

Open-ended questions requiring written responses. AI-graded for accuracy and fluency.

A2-C2 Best for: writing practice, critical thinking

Discussion Questions

Thought-provoking questions for extended written or spoken responses.

B1-C2 Best for: fluency development, opinion expression

Error Correction

Find and fix grammatical or vocabulary mistakes in sentences.

A2-C2 Best for: grammar accuracy, proofreading skills

Odd One Out

Identify which word doesn't belong in a group and explain why.

A2-C1 Best for: vocabulary categorization, reasoning

Matching Halves

Connect sentence beginnings with their correct endings.

A2-C2 Best for: grammar structures, collocations

Word Order

Arrange scrambled words into grammatically correct sentences.

A1-B2 Best for: syntax practice, sentence structure

Gap Text (Cloze)

A full text with multiple words removed. Students fill gaps using context clues.

B1-C2 Best for: advanced reading, grammar in context

Negative Prefixes

Add correct negative prefixes (un-, in-, im-, ir-, dis-) to words.

B1-C1 Best for: word formation, vocabulary expansion

Categorization

Sort words or phrases into semantic or grammatical categories.

A1-C1 Best for: vocabulary organization, classification skills

Complete Word

Fill in missing letters to complete partially shown words.

A1-B1 Best for: spelling, vocabulary recall

Paraphrasing

Rewrite sentences using different words while preserving the meaning. AI-graded.

B1-C2 Best for: productive vocabulary, writing flexibility

Sentence Transformation

Rewrite sentences using a given word or structure. Common in Cambridge exams.

B1-C2 Best for: exam preparation, grammar mastery

Synonyms Matching

Match words with their synonyms from a provided list.

A2-C2 Best for: vocabulary expansion, nuance awareness

Antonyms Matching

Match words with their opposites.

A2-C1 Best for: vocabulary range, word relationships
