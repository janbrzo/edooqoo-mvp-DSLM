Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create worksheets for specific professions?

Yes. Set the topic to your student's profession: "IT English," "medical English," "legal English," "marketing English." The AI generates vocabulary and scenarios relevant to their field.

How do I handle mixed-level adult groups?

Generate differentiated worksheets at different CEFR levels on the same topic. All students discuss the same theme but with exercises matched to their individual level.
