Listening Activities by Level

A1-A2: Foundation Listening

  • Short audio (30-60 seconds): greetings, introductions, simple directions, daily routines
  • Clear speech, slow pace, common vocabulary
  • Pre-listening vocabulary with visual support
  • Listen and circle/tick answers
  • Multiple listenings encouraged

B1-B2: Interactive Listening

  • Medium audio (1-3 minutes): conversations, interviews, presentations, news reports
  • Natural speech pace with some connected speech features
  • Note-taking while listening
  • Inference and opinion questions
  • Fill in the Blanks with functional language

C1-C2: Advanced Listening

  • Longer audio (3-5 minutes): lectures, debates, complex narratives
  • Fast pace, idiomatic language, multiple speakers
  • Identifying speaker attitude and purpose
  • Critical evaluation of arguments
  • Detailed gap-fill with academic vocabulary

Tips for Effective Listening Lessons

  • Pre-listening: Activate background knowledge, pre-teach 3-5 key words
  • First listen: Focus on gist (general understanding) — "What is the audio about?"
  • Second listen: Focus on details — complete specific tasks
  • Post-listening: Discuss content, practice new vocabulary, connect to speaking/writing
  • Allow replays: In homework mode, students can replay as many times as needed

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the AI-generated audio sound like?

Edooqoo uses high-quality text-to-speech technology that produces clear, natural-sounding English. The audio is excellent for focused listening practice and comprehension development. For accent exposure, teachers can supplement with authentic materials.

Can I assign listening exercises as homework?

Yes. Assign audio exercises through the homework system. Students access them online, listen at their own pace, and complete the tasks. Fill in the Blanks, True/False, and Multiple Choice are graded automatically by AI. Teachers review and add feedback.

How many audio exercises should I include in a worksheet?

For a focused listening lesson, 2-3 audio exercises per worksheet work well (e.g., Listening Comprehension + Fill in Blanks + True/False on the same audio). You can also combine 1 audio exercise with reading/grammar exercises for an integrated skills worksheet.
