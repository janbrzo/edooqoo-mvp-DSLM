Information Technology

Vocabulary: agile methodology, sprint, deployment, debugging, cloud computing, cybersecurity, API, UX/UI. Exercises: dialogue practice for sprint planning meetings, reading comprehension with tech articles, paraphrasing technical documentation for non-technical stakeholders.

Marketing & Advertising

Human Resources

Legal

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. When generating a worksheet, specify the industry context (e.g., "IT project management" or "pharmaceutical sales") and the AI creates exercises with relevant terminology and scenarios.

Business English typically ranges from B1 to C2. Edooqoo calibrates vocabulary complexity and task demands. B1 covers basic workplace communication; C1-C2 handles negotiations, formal reports, and nuanced professional discourse.

Can I track business vocabulary progress?

Yes. The nano-skill tracking system monitors business vocabulary mastery. Flashcards with spaced repetition help students retain industry-specific terms long-term.

Is Business English homework effective?

Yes. Assigning business writing exercises as homework (email writing, report summaries) with AI grading provides valuable practice between lessons. The AI evaluates register, appropriateness, and clarity.
