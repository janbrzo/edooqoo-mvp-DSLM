    What it does in the workflow

    Public booking page

    Students book lessons through your personal link. No back-and-forth messages. Slots update in real-time.

    Google Calendar sync

    Bidirectional sync with Google Calendar. Per-status colors. Auto-generate Google Meet links for online lessons.

    Reschedule system

    Students request reschedule → you confirm or reject. Calendar updates automatically. No manual coordination.

    Student GCal sync

    Students sync lessons to their own Google Calendar with customizable colors and reminder settings.

    How it works

    1

    Set your availability

    Define weekly recurring slots (e.g., Monday 9:00–10:00, Thursday 14:00–15:00). Set buffer time between lessons.

    2

    Share your booking page

    Send students your public booking link. They see available slots and book directly — no messages needed.

    3

    Manage bookings

    Confirm or reject bookings. Handle reschedule requests. Use bulk actions for batch operations. Everything syncs to Google Calendar.

    4

    Teach and track

    Mark lessons as completed or no-show. Lesson cadence and attendance context can support future planning.
