    What flashcards add to the learning loop

    A broad vocabulary goal becomes concrete when each word or phrase is tracked as a card-level item. SM-2 progress then shows which vocabulary needs more exposure between lessons.

    Word/card item

    A card represents a concrete vocabulary item, phrase, or expression.

    Retention progress

    Reviews update due dates, quality rating, response time, and mistake counts.

    Prep context

    The next lesson can keep vocabulary continuity visible for teacher review.

    What it does in the workflow

    SM-2 scheduling logic

    Spaced-repetition scheduling based on SM-2 logic. Cards the student struggles with appear more often.

    Auto-generated from worksheets

    One click creates a flashcard set from any worksheet. Vocabulary, key phrases, grammar patterns — extracted automatically.

    Word-level skill context

    Each word/card can act as a vocabulary nano-skill context item while SM-2 progress records retention behavior.

    Student Hub access

    Students study through their Hub — no app needed. Share via link. Track progress in real-time.

    How it works

    1

    Generate a worksheet

    Create any worksheet with vocabulary, grammar, or mixed exercises.

    2

    Create flashcards with one click

    Click "Create Flashcards" — Edooqoo extracts key vocabulary and creates a set automatically. You can edit, add, or remove cards.

    3

    Share with the student

    Send a link or let students access through Student Hub. No account needed — just email-based access.

    4

    SM-2 schedules reviews

    Students rate difficulty (Again/Hard/Good/Easy), and the system schedules harder cards more frequently.
