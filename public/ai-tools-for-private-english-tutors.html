Frequently Asked Questions

How many students can I manage?

Unlimited. There's no cap on the number of students you can add to your Edooqoo account. Each student gets their own profile, worksheet history, flashcard sets, and progress data.

Can students book lessons directly?

Yes. You get a public booking page link to share with students. They enter their email, browse your available time slots, and book — including weekly recurring lessons. Everything syncs with Google Calendar.

How does the AI personalize worksheets?

The AI uses Student Knowledge entries (notes about strengths, weaknesses, interests) and DSLM skill data from previous worksheets and homework. Each worksheet is tailored to the specific student.

Can students study between lessons?

Yes. Through the Student Hub portal, students can browse shared worksheets, study flashcards with spaced repetition, complete homework assignments, and manage their lesson bookings — all independently.

What's the best plan for private tutors?

Most private tutors start with the Side-Gig plan ($9/mo, 15 worksheets/month) for up to 15 students. For 15+ active students, the Full-Time plan ($19/mo, 30 worksheets/month) provides more capacity. All plans include homework, flashcards, calendar, and progress tracking.
