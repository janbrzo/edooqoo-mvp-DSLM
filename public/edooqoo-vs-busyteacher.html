The Core Difference: Generated vs. Downloaded

BusyTeacher: The Library Approach

BusyTeacher has 28,000+ worksheets uploaded by teachers worldwide. You search by topic or grammar point, preview, and download a PDF. Advantages: free, large variety, sometimes creative activities. Disadvantages: inconsistent quality, often not matching your student's exact level, no answer keys, outdated content, no interactive features.

Edooqoo: The AI Generation Approach

Edooqoo generates a unique worksheet every time based on your specifications: topic, CEFR level, grammar focus, exercise types, and even student interests. The result is always level-appropriate, always has answer keys, and can be assigned as interactive online homework with AI grading.

When to Use Edooqoo

  • You need worksheets tailored to your student's specific level and interests
  • You want reliable quality and answer keys every time
  • You need interactive online homework with automatic grading
  • You want to track student progress over time
  • You teach 1-on-1 and need personalized materials for each student

When to Use BusyTeacher

  • You need free resources and have zero budget
  • You want creative activity ideas from other teachers
  • You need a quick printable worksheet for a general topic
  • You're looking for inspiration for lesson planning

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BusyTeacher really free?

Yes, BusyTeacher is completely free to use and download from. The worksheets are uploaded by volunteer teachers. However, the quality varies significantly — some worksheets are excellent, others have errors or unclear instructions. You spend time searching and evaluating quality.

Can I use both?

Yes. Some teachers browse BusyTeacher for inspiration and activity ideas, then use Edooqoo to generate polished, level-appropriate worksheets based on those ideas. This combines the creativity of the teaching community with the precision of AI generation.

Which saves more time?

Edooqoo saves significantly more time. With BusyTeacher, you spend 15-30 minutes searching, previewing, and evaluating worksheets — and often need to modify them. With Edooqoo, you describe what you need and get a ready-to-use worksheet in under 60 seconds.
