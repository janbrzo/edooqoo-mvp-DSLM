Best Exercise Types

3. Vocabulary Exercises

4. Listening Comprehension

Grammar Through Technology

  • Comparatives: "This phone is faster than..." "The latest model has a better camera."
  • Present Perfect: "Technology has changed how we communicate."
  • Future predictions: "AI will transform healthcare." "By 2030, most cars will be electric."
  • Passive Voice: "The app was downloaded by millions." "Data is stored in the cloud."
  • Conditionals: "If you click that link, you might get a virus."
