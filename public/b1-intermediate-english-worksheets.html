B1 Intermediate English Worksheets — AI Generated

Edooqoo generates B1 intermediate English worksheets personalized for each student in under 60 seconds. The AI creates exercises covering present perfect, first conditional, passive voice, modals, and opinion expression — all at CEFR B1 difficulty with appropriate vocabulary and text complexity.

CEFR B1 is a critical threshold in language learning — the "independent user" level. B1 learners can understand the main points of clear standard input on familiar matters regularly encountered in work, school, leisure. They can deal with most situations likely to arise whilst travelling and can produce simple connected text on topics which are familiar or of personal interest.

B1 is the most common level among adult ESL/EFL learners worldwide. Edooqoo generates materials that bridge the gap between basic communication (A2) and confident fluency (B2), with particular focus on accuracy in verb tenses, opinion expression, and paragraph-level reading.

Key B1 Grammar Topics

  • Present perfect (experience, duration, unfinished time)
  • Present perfect vs. past simple
  • First conditional
  • Passive voice (present and past simple)
  • Modal verbs (must, should, might, could)
  • Reported speech (basic)
  • Relative clauses (who, which, that)
  • Used to / would for past habits
