Vocabulary Exercise Generator for English Teachers

Edooqoo generates vocabulary exercises for English lessons instantly using AI. The platform creates topic-specific vocabulary exercises including matching, fill in the blanks, categorization, synonyms matching, antonyms matching, odd one out, complete word, and negative prefixes — all calibrated to CEFR levels A1 through C2.

Effective vocabulary instruction requires multiple exposures in varied contexts. Research shows that learners need 10-16 encounters with a new word before it enters long-term memory. Edooqoo addresses this by generating diverse exercise types that practice the same vocabulary from different angles, and by creating flashcards with SM-2 spaced repetition for long-term retention.

Each vocabulary exercise is personalized. The AI knows which words the student has already learned (via the Student Knowledge base and DSLM skill tracking) and generates exercises that introduce new vocabulary while reinforcing recently learned items.

Vocabulary Exercise Types in Edooqoo

  • Matching Exercise — Match words to definitions, translations, or pictures
  • Synonyms Matching — Match words with their synonyms from a list
  • Antonyms Matching — Match words with their opposites
  • Categorization — Sort words into correct categories (e.g., food groups, emotions)
  • Odd One Out — Identify which word doesn't belong and explain why
  • Complete Word — Fill in missing letters based on definitions or context
  • Negative Prefixes — Add correct prefixes (un-, in-, im-, ir-, dis-) to words
  • Fill in the Blanks — Use vocabulary in context sentences
  • Smart Flashcards — Auto-generated from worksheet vocabulary with SM-2 spaced repetition

Vocabulary + Flashcards Workflow

  1. Generate a vocabulary-focused worksheet with topic words
  2. Student completes exercises to learn words in context
  3. Add vocabulary to flashcard set (one-click or automatic)
  4. Student reviews flashcards using SM-2 spaced repetition via Student Hub
  5. DSLM tracks vocabulary mastery and suggests review when needed

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I generate vocabulary for specific topics?

Yes. Specify any topic — travel, business meetings, healthcare, technology, cooking — and the AI generates vocabulary exercises with relevant words and phrases at the selected CEFR level.

How does Edooqoo track vocabulary learning?

The DSLM tracks vocabulary mastery at the nano-skill level. It records which words students know, which they struggle with, and recommends review. Flashcards use SM-2 spaced repetition to optimize long-term retention.

Can I create flashcards from vocabulary exercises?

Yes. You can add individual words or entire vocabulary lists to flashcard sets with one click. Students study via the Student Hub portal using spaced repetition.

What CEFR levels are supported?

All levels from A1 (Beginner) to C2 (Proficiency). The AI adjusts word frequency, complexity, and context difficulty accordingly.

Can vocabulary exercises be assigned as homework?

Yes. Assign any vocabulary exercises as homework with deadlines and email notifications. Students complete them online, and the AI grades their responses automatically.
