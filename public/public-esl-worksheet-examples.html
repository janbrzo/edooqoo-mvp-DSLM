Try It: Reported speech Task for Adult Learners (B2)

A short task in the style Edooqoo produces, set in a real adult context: summarising a meeting for a manager. Use it as is in your next lesson, or generate an editable version tuned to your learner.

  1. "We will deliver on Monday." The supplier said they ____ deliver on Monday.
  2. "Are you available?" She asked me if I ____ available.
  3. "Send the file." He told me ____ the file.
Answer key

1. would
2. was
3. to send

Common Adult Learner Errors and How to Fix Them

What learners sayCorrect formTeaching note
He said me that the price was too high.He told me that the price was too high.say takes no personal object; tell does.
She asked me where was the office.She asked me where the office was.Reported questions use statement word order.

If one of these errors keeps returning after practice, treat it as a repair point rather than a reason to move on.
