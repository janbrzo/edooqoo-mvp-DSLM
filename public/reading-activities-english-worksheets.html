Reading Activities by CEFR Level

A1-A2: Foundation Reading

  • Short texts (100-200 words): notices, emails, simple descriptions, menus
  • Pre-reading vocabulary introduction with Matching or Fill in the Blanks
  • Simple comprehension questions (who, what, where, when)
  • True/False with straightforward statements
  • Picture-text matching for support

B1-B2: Interactive Reading

  • Medium texts (300-500 words): articles, blog posts, stories, reviews
  • Inference questions (reading between the lines)
  • Vocabulary-in-context exercises (guess meaning from context)
  • Summary writing after reading
  • Reading + Discussion Questions for integrated skills

C1-C2: Critical Reading

  • Longer texts (500-800 words): academic articles, opinion pieces, complex narratives
  • Analysis of author's purpose, tone, and bias
  • Multiple texts comparison
  • Gap text (Cloze) with advanced vocabulary
  • Paraphrasing exercises from the text

Reading Sub-Skills to Develop

  1. Skimming — Getting the general idea quickly (supported by Multiple Choice about gist)
  2. Scanning — Finding specific information (supported by True/False, detail questions)
  3. Detailed reading — Understanding every part (supported by comprehension questions)
  4. Inference — Understanding implied meaning (supported by "Why..." questions)
  5. Vocabulary from context — Guessing meaning of unknown words (supported by cloze exercises)

How to Structure a Reading Lesson

  1. Pre-reading (5 min) — Activate background knowledge, pre-teach key vocabulary (use Matching/Fill in the Blanks)
  2. First reading (5 min) — Skim for gist, answer 1-2 general questions
  3. Second reading (10 min) — Detailed reading with comprehension questions
  4. Vocabulary focus (5 min) — Work with new words from the text
  5. Post-reading (5 min) — Discussion, opinion, connection to personal experience
