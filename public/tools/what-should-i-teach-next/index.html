    Local decision tool for adult 1:1 tutors

    What Should I Teach Next?

    Enter bounded teaching evidence. The tool applies transparent rules and returns Repair, Continue, or Advance with a usable next-lesson brief.

    Runs locally. No AI, account, student name, email, notes, or worksheet-generation call.

    Decision rules

    Repair

    Use when the previous objective was not mastered or a recurring error blocks the communication goal.

    Continue

    Use when evidence is incomplete, control is partial, or success still depends on prompts.

    Advance

    Use after accurate independent performance with no established recurring target error.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does this tool use AI or upload student data?

    No. The decision is calculated locally in the browser with fixed rules. The form contains no student name, email, notes, or free-text personal data.

    What is the difference between Repair, Continue, and Advance?

    Repair addresses a blocking gap, Continue reduces support or gathers stronger evidence for the current objective, and Advance transfers secure independent performance to a harder context.

    Is the result a complete lesson plan?

    No. It is a bounded teaching decision with an objective, activity structure, worksheet brief, and evidence criteria for an adult one-to-one lesson.
