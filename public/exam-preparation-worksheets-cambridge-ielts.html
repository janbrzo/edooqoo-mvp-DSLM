Exam Preparation Worksheets — Cambridge & IELTS

Edooqoo generates exam preparation worksheets for Cambridge English exams and IELTS using AI. The platform creates exercises that mirror exam formats — Use of English transformations, reading comprehension, listening tasks, and writing practice — at the correct CEFR level for each exam.

Exam preparation is one of the most demanding areas of English teaching. Students need extensive practice with exam-format exercises, but creating new practice tests is extremely time-consuming. KET (A2 Key) Preparation

KET tests basic English at A2 level. Edooqoo generates A2-calibrated exercises focusing on everyday vocabulary, simple grammar (past simple, present continuous, comparatives), and short reading texts about familiar topics. Key exercise types: Multiple Choice for reading, Fill in the Blanks for grammar, Matching for vocabulary, and Listening Comprehension with slow, clear AI speech.

PET (B1 Preliminary) Preparation

PET tests intermediate English at B1 level. Edooqoo creates B1 exercises with present perfect, first conditional, and modals. Reading texts are 200-350 words on familiar topics. Sentence Transformation exercises practice the "rewrite using the word given" format central to PET. Dialogue Practice exercises prepare students for the speaking paper.

FCE (B2 First) Preparation

FCE is the most popular Cambridge exam. Edooqoo generates B2 exercises targeting Use of English skills: Sentence Transformation (key word transformation), Error Correction (open cloze), Fill in the Blanks (word formation), and Reading Comprehension (multiple matching, gapped text). The AI produces texts with the complexity and topic range expected at B2 level.

CAE (C1 Advanced) Preparation

CAE requires sophisticated language skills. Edooqoo creates C1 exercises with advanced grammar (inversion, cleft sentences), complex vocabulary (collocations, phrasal verbs), and longer reading texts (500-700 words). Sentence Transformation exercises at C1 level test nuanced meaning preservation. Discussion Questions prepare students for the C1 speaking paper with complex argumentation.

CPE (C2 Proficiency) Preparation

CPE is the highest Cambridge exam. Edooqoo generates C2 exercises focusing on stylistic mastery, idiomatic precision, and register flexibility. Paraphrasing exercises require nuanced rewording. Reading Comprehension uses literary and academic texts. Discussion Questions test the ability to construct sophisticated arguments with native-like fluency.

IELTS Academic & General Preparation

IELTS scores map to CEFR B1-C2. Edooqoo generates exercises mirroring IELTS formats: Reading Comprehension with True/False/Not Given questions, Listening Comprehension with note-completion tasks, Fill in the Blanks for summary completion, and Discussion Questions for speaking practice. For Academic IELTS, the AI generates texts from scientific, sociological, and historical sources. For General Training, it uses workplace and social context texts.

Creating Mock Tests with AI

While Edooqoo doesn't generate complete timed exam papers, you can create effective mock test sections:

  1. Reading paper — Generate 2-3 Reading Comprehension exercises at the target level with mixed question types (multiple choice, true/false, matching).
  2. Use of English — Combine Sentence Transformation, Fill in the Blanks (open cloze), and Error Correction exercises.
  3. Listening paper — Use Listening Comprehension, Multiple Choice (Audio), and Fill in the Blanks (Audio) exercises.
  4. Speaking preparation — Use Discussion Questions and Describe Picture exercises for speaking practice with AI evaluation.

Assign as homework for timed practice at home, or use in Live Session mode for supervised mock test conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Edooqoo create official exam papers?

No. Edooqoo creates practice exercises in exam-relevant formats and at the correct difficulty level. For official past papers, use Cambridge Assessment publications. Edooqoo supplements these with unlimited additional practice.

Can I track exam readiness?

Yes. The nano-skill tracking system monitors skills relevant to each exam (reading, writing, Use of English, listening, speaking) and shows mastery trends. You can identify weak areas that need more practice before the exam.

How many practice worksheets can I create?

It depends on your plan. Free accounts get 2 worksheets. Subscription plans offer 15-90 worksheets per month. Each worksheet can contain up to 12 exercises.

Can students practice at home?

Yes. Assign exam practice exercises as homework. Students complete them online, AI grades the responses, and you review the results. This provides valuable exam practice between lessons.

What about TOEFL and TOEIC?

While Edooqoo's exercise types are most closely aligned with Cambridge and IELTS formats, many exercise types (reading comprehension, listening, multiple choice) are also relevant for TOEFL and TOEIC preparation.

Can I combine exam prep with general English teaching?

Yes. Many teachers dedicate part of each lesson to exam-specific practice and part to general English improvement. Edooqoo makes this easy — generate one worksheet with exam-format exercises and another with general conversation or vocabulary exercises.

How do I know when a student is ready for the exam?

Use the nano-skill mastery tracking. When a student consistently achieves 75%+ mastery across all relevant skills at the target CEFR level, they're likely ready. The AI suggestions feature also indicates when a student has covered the key skill areas.

Are there specific prompts for exam preparation?

Yes. The Prompt Library includes ready-to-use prompts specifically designed for Cambridge and IELTS exam preparation. These specify the correct CEFR level, exam-relevant topics, and appropriate exercise types.

Can I generate exam prep for young learners?

Edooqoo is designed for adult learners (16+). For Cambridge Young Learners exams (Starters, Movers, Flyers), the content topics may not be age-appropriate. The exercises work well for teen and adult exam candidates at KET (A2) and above.

How does AI-generated exam practice compare to official past papers?

AI-generated exercises provide unlimited fresh practice in exam-relevant formats. Official past papers provide the most authentic experience. The best approach combines both: use official papers for timed mock tests and Edooqoo for daily practice between mocks.
