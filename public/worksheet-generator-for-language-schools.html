Frequently Asked Questions

Can multiple teachers use Edooqoo at the same school?

Each teacher has their own Edooqoo account with their own students and worksheets. There's no limit on the number of teachers at one school. Each account is independent with its own subscription.

What's the best plan for a school with many students?

Most school teachers use the Full-Time plans ($19-49/mo for 30-90 worksheets/month). For very high-volume needs, additional token packs can be purchased separately.

Can students access materials at home?

Yes. Through the Student Hub portal, students access shared worksheets, complete homework, study flashcards, and manage lesson bookings — all from their browser without needing a teacher account.

Does Edooqoo replace textbooks?

Edooqoo supplements or replaces supplementary materials. Teachers can generate topic-specific worksheets that perfectly align with their textbook's scope and sequence, filling gaps or providing extra practice.

How does homework grading save time?

AI automatically evaluates open-ended answers (sentence transformation, paraphrasing, discussion questions) and pre-fills mastery scores. Teachers review and adjust — spending 2 minutes instead of 20 per student.
