    Teaching decision criteria

    Use this page when
    Use phrasal verbs when a learner repeatedly encounters a small set in meetings, messages, media, or informal professional conversation.
    Do not use it when
    Do not teach unrelated alphabetical lists; exclude items the learner is unlikely to retrieve in the next month.
    Adult 1:1 task example
    The learner uses six selected phrasal verbs to hand over a project, clarify ownership, and describe what must be followed up.
    Evidence of quality
    The learner retrieves the item with the correct particle, object pattern, register, and meaning in a relevant sentence rather than only matching a definition.
