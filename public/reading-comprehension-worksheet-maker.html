Reading Comprehension Worksheet Maker for ESL

Edooqoo is an AI-powered reading comprehension worksheet maker designed for ESL and EFL teachers. It generates original reading passages with comprehension questions tailored to any CEFR level from A1 to C2. Each text is created specifically for your student's level, topic interests, and learning goals — no recycled content from textbooks.

Reading comprehension is a cornerstone of English language learning. According to research in second language acquisition, extensive reading at the appropriate difficulty level significantly improves vocabulary acquisition, grammar intuition, and overall fluency. Edooqoo ensures every reading passage matches the student's exact proficiency level.

The AI generates texts on any topic — from everyday situations for beginners to complex academic or business texts for advanced learners. Questions test literal comprehension, inference, vocabulary in context, and critical thinking. All answers can be graded automatically by AI.

How Edooqoo Creates Reading Comprehension Worksheets

  1. Choose the topic — "Climate change," "job interviews," "travel in Japan" — any topic works. The AI creates an original text.
  2. Set the CEFR level — The AI calibrates text length, vocabulary complexity, sentence structure, and question difficulty to match A1-C2.
  3. Select question types — Combine reading comprehension with follow-up exercises like discussion questions, vocabulary matching, or true/false.
  4. Share instantly — Students read and answer online, or download as HTML/PDF for offline use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the reading texts original or copied?

All texts are 100% AI-generated and original. Each passage is created specifically for your student's level and topic, so you never get recycled textbook content.

Can I combine reading with other exercises?

Yes. A typical worksheet might include a reading passage followed by comprehension questions, vocabulary matching, discussion questions, and sentence transformation — all in one worksheet.

How does AI grade reading comprehension answers?

For factual questions, the AI compares against the answer key. For open-ended questions (inference, opinion, discussion), the AI evaluates the quality of the response and provides a mastery score (0-100) that teachers can review and adjust.

Can students complete reading exercises online?

Yes. Students read the text and answer questions interactively in their browser. Teachers can see responses in real-time during live sessions or review them after homework submission.

What topics can I use for reading comprehension?

Any topic. Popular choices include travel, technology, health, environment, business, culture, daily life, science, and current events. The AI adapts vocabulary and complexity to match both the topic and the student's CEFR level.
