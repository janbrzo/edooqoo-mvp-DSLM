Spaced Repetition Flashcards for ESL Students

Edooqoo creates spaced repetition flashcards for ESL students using SM-2 scheduling logic. Teachers generate flashcard sets from worksheet vocabulary with one click or create cards manually. Students study through the Student Hub portal, and supported review activity can contribute vocabulary signals for future prep.

Spaced repetition schedules vocabulary review across multiple sessions instead of leaving review to memory. The SM-2 algorithm (SuperMemo 2) calculates review intervals based on how well a student remembers each word. Words that are difficult are reviewed more frequently; words that are easy are spaced further apart.

Unlike standalone flashcard apps (Anki, Quizlet), Edooqoo's flashcards are integrated into the teaching workflow. Vocabulary from worksheets flows directly into flashcard sets. Flashcard performance feeds into the DSLM progress tracking. Teachers see which vocabulary students retain and which needs re-teaching.

Try It: Vocabulary in context Task for Adult Learners (B1)

A short task in the style Edooqoo produces, set in a real adult context: workplace collocations. Use it as is in your next lesson, or generate an editable version tuned to your learner.

Common Adult Learner Errors and How to Fix Them

What learners sayCorrect formTeaching note
We must do a decision.We must make a decision.Collocations are learned as fixed chunks, not word by word.
I want to make a training next month.I want to run a training session next month.Direct translation produces the wrong verb.

If one of these errors keeps returning after practice, treat it as a repair point rather than a reason to move on.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SM-2 algorithm?

SM-2 (SuperMemo 2) is a spaced repetition algorithm that calculates when each flashcard should be reviewed. It tracks an "easiness factor" per card: easy cards are shown less frequently, difficult cards more frequently.

Can students study on their phone?

Yes. The Student Hub and flashcard interface work on any device with a web browser: smartphone, tablet, or computer. No app installation needed.

Can I see what my students are studying?

Yes. Teachers see flashcard sets per student, review statistics, and vocabulary mastery levels. The DSLM integrates flashcard performance into overall progress tracking.

Can I create flashcards without worksheets?

Yes. You can manually create flashcard sets and add individual cards with front text, back text, and optional example sentences. This is useful for ad-hoc vocabulary practice outside of lessons.

Is bidirectional study available?

Yes. Students can study cards in both directions, seeing the word and recalling the definition, or seeing the definition and recalling the word. This strengthens both recognition and production.
