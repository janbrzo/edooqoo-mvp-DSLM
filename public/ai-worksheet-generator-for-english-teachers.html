How Edooqoo's AI Worksheet Generator Works

  1. Select your student — Choose the student from your roster. Edooqoo uses their learning profile, CEFR level, strengths, weaknesses, and interests to personalize content.
  2. Configure the worksheet — Set the topic (e.g., "travel vocabulary"), grammar focus (e.g., "past simple vs. present perfect"), CEFR level (A1-C2), and select from 29 exercise types.
  3. AI generates in seconds — The AI creates a complete, ready-to-use worksheet with exercises, answer keys, vocabulary lists, and audio/picture elements if selected.
  4. Share and track — Share the worksheet via a permanent link, assign as homework, use in a live session, or download as HTML/PDF.

Tips for Better AI-Generated Worksheets

  1. Be specific with topics — "Business email writing for IT project managers" produces better results than just "business English." The more context you provide, the more relevant the content.
  2. Add student context — Fill in Student Knowledge with interests, job title, hobbies, and learning goals. The AI uses all of this information to personalize exercises.
  3. Mix exercise types — Combine receptive skills (reading, listening) with productive skills (writing via paraphrasing/discussion, speaking via describe picture). A balanced worksheet improves engagement.
  4. Use grammar focus strategically — Specify a grammar point to ensure exercises practice that structure. Leave it empty for topic-focused worksheets where grammar varies naturally.
  5. Leverage the Welcome Test — Students who complete the 49-question assessment get significantly more personalized worksheets because the AI knows their exact skill profile.
  6. Add vocabulary to flashcards — After generating a worksheet, add key vocabulary to the student's flashcard set. The SM-2 spaced repetition algorithm ensures long-term retention.
  7. Review AI worksheet suggestions — After students complete homework and the progress tracking updates, check the AI-generated suggestions for your next worksheet. They target specific skill gaps automatically.
