Try It: Present Perfect Task for Adult Learners (B1)

A short task in the style Edooqoo produces, set in a real adult context: project status updates and career experience. Use it as is in your next lesson, or generate an editable version tuned to your learner.

  1. I ____ (work) here since 2019.
  2. ____ you ever ____ (present) to the board?
  3. We ____ (not / receive) the final figures yet.
Answer key

1. have worked
2. Have / presented
3. haven't received

Common Adult Learner Errors and How to Fix Them

What learners sayCorrect formTeaching note
I live here since two years.I've lived here for two years.Duration up to now needs Present Perfect plus for (a period) or since (a start point).
I have seen him last Monday.I saw him last Monday.A finished time reference blocks Present Perfect.

If one of these errors keeps returning after practice, treat it as a repair point rather than a reason to move on.
