Frequently Asked Questions

Is ISLCollective free?

ISLCollective offers free access with daily download limits. Premium removes limits. However, all materials are user-uploaded — quality and accuracy vary. Edooqoo offers 2 free AI-generated worksheets, then plans from $9/mo with consistent AI quality.

Can ISLCollective generate worksheets with AI?

No. ISLCollective is a sharing platform where teachers upload materials. There is no AI generation. Edooqoo generates unique, personalized worksheets using AI in under 60 seconds with 29 exercise types.

Does ISLCollective track student progress?

No. ISLCollective is focused on material distribution, not student management. Edooqoo tracks student skills at the nano-skill level, provides mastery trends, and gives AI-powered suggestions for future lessons.

Can I assign homework on ISLCollective?

ISLCollective doesn't have a homework system. Edooqoo lets you assign any worksheet as homework with deadlines, email notifications, and automatic AI grading of open-ended answers.

Which is better for private English tutors?

For tutors who need personalized materials for each student, Edooqoo is the better choice. It generates worksheets tailored to each student's level and interests, tracks progress, manages scheduling, and provides a complete Student Hub portal for independent learning.
