Multiple Choice Quiz Generator for English Lessons

Edooqoo generates multiple choice quizzes for English lessons in seconds using AI. The platform creates MCQ exercises covering grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension, listening, and picture-based questions for all CEFR levels (A1-C2). Each quiz is personalized to the student's proficiency and learning goals.

Multiple choice questions are one of the most versatile exercise types in English language teaching. They provide instant, objective assessment of student knowledge and are ideal for both formative and summative evaluation. Edooqoo generates 3-4 carefully crafted options per question, with plausible distractors that test real understanding rather than guessing.

Edooqoo offers 3 variants of multiple choice: standard text-based MCQ, audio-based MCQ (students listen to AI-generated speech and answer), and picture-based MCQ (students look at AI-generated images and select answers). All variants include automatic grading.

Multiple Choice Variants in Edooqoo

  • Multiple Choice (Text) — Standard grammar and vocabulary questions with 3-4 options
  • Multiple Choice (Audio) — Listen to AI-generated audio, then select the correct answer
  • Multiple Choice (Picture) — View an AI-generated image and answer questions about it

Frequently Asked Questions

How many options per question?

Edooqoo generates 3-4 options per multiple choice question, with one correct answer and 2-3 carefully crafted distractors that test common mistakes at the selected CEFR level.

Can I use MCQ for listening exercises?

Yes. Edooqoo's "Multiple Choice (Audio)" exercise type generates AI speech and then asks multiple choice questions about the audio content. Students listen and select answers.

Are MCQ exercises auto-graded?

Yes. All multiple choice exercises are automatically graded with instant results. Teachers can review scores in the worksheet view or homework review panel.

Can I combine MCQ with other exercise types?

Yes. You can include multiple choice alongside fill in the blanks, reading comprehension, matching, and any of the other 29 exercise types in a single worksheet.

Is MCQ suitable for exam preparation?

Absolutely. Multiple choice is a standard format in Cambridge exams (FCE, CAE, CPE), IELTS, and TOEFL. Edooqoo generates MCQ exercises that mirror exam-style question formats.
