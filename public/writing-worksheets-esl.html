How Edooqoo Supports Writing Development

Pre-Writing Exercises

  • Vocabulary building — Fill in the Blanks, Matching, and Categorization introduce topic vocabulary
  • Grammar practice — Sentence Transformation and Error Correction target structures needed for writing
  • Model texts — Reading Comprehension exercises expose students to well-written examples of the target genre

Controlled Writing Practice

  • Word Order — Reconstruct sentences from scrambled words (sentence-level accuracy)
  • Gap Text (Cloze) — Complete paragraphs with missing words (cohesion and coherence)
  • Sentence Transformation — Rewrite sentences using different structures (grammatical range)
  • Paraphrasing — Express the same idea in different words (lexical flexibility)

Guided Writing

  • Answer Questions — Write responses to guided questions about a topic
  • Dialogue completion — Write appropriate replies in conversation contexts
  • Error Correction — Develop proofreading skills essential for good writing

Writing Sub-Skills to Develop

  1. Accuracy — Grammar, spelling, punctuation (supported by Error Correction, Fill in Blanks)
  2. Range — Variety of vocabulary and structures (Sentence Transformation, Paraphrasing)
  3. Coherence — Logical flow of ideas (Gap Text, Reading + Writing tasks)
  4. Cohesion — Linking words and reference (Fill in Blanks targeting connectors)
  5. Appropriacy — Register, formality, genre conventions (Model texts + practice)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Edooqoo grade student writing?

Edooqoo's AI can grade structured exercises (Fill in the Blanks, Error Correction, Sentence Transformation) automatically. For free writing tasks, teachers review and provide feedback. The homework system lets you assign writing exercises with deadlines and track completion.

How do I combine Edooqoo exercises for a writing lesson?

Build a worksheet with 3-4 exercise types: 1) Vocabulary introduction (Matching or Fill in the Blanks), 2) Model text analysis (Reading Comprehension), 3) Controlled practice (Sentence Transformation), 4) Guided production (Answer Questions). This scaffolded approach prepares students for independent writing.

What exercises work best for exam writing preparation?

For Cambridge and IELTS writing: Sentence Transformation (Use of English Part 4), Paraphrasing (IELTS Task 1 rephrasing), Error Correction (proofreading skills), and Discussion Questions adapted to essay topics. Edooqoo generates exercises aligned with exam formats.
