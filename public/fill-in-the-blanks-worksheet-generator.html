Fill in the Blanks Worksheet Generator for English Teachers

Edooqoo generates fill in the blanks worksheets for English lessons in under 60 seconds. The AI creates personalized gap-fill exercises targeting grammar, vocabulary, and collocations at any CEFR level from A1 to C2. Each worksheet is customized to the student's proficiency level, learning goals, and interests.

Fill in the blanks (also called gap-fill or cloze exercises) are among the most effective exercise types for English language learning. They test active recall, contextual understanding, and grammatical accuracy simultaneously. Edooqoo's AI generates these exercises with carefully selected gaps that target specific grammar points or vocabulary items.

Teachers can combine fill in the blanks with 28 other exercise types in a single worksheet, including reading comprehension, multiple choice, matching, and audio-based exercises. The AI automatically generates answer keys and can grade student responses — including open-ended variations — using AI evaluation.

How Edooqoo Creates Fill in the Blanks Worksheets

  1. Select your student — Edooqoo uses their CEFR level, known vocabulary, and skill gaps to create appropriately challenging gaps.
  2. Set the topic and grammar focus — For example, "business meetings" with "present perfect vs. past simple." The AI generates sentences within this context.
  3. Choose exercise count — Select how many fill in the blanks exercises to include (up to 12 exercises per worksheet).
  4. Generate and share — The AI creates the exercises with answer keys. Share via link, assign as homework, or use in a live session.

Types of Fill in the Blanks Exercises

  • Grammar-focused gaps — Test specific structures: verb tenses, articles, prepositions, conditionals
  • Vocabulary gaps — Students recall topic-specific words from context clues
  • Collocation gaps — Complete common word combinations (make/do, take/bring)
  • Audio-based fill in the blanks — Students listen to AI-generated audio and fill missing words in a transcript
  • Gap Text (Cloze) — Fill gaps in longer passages testing contextual understanding
