Listening Comprehension Exercises for ESL — AI Generated

Edooqoo generates listening comprehension exercises for ESL and EFL students using AI-generated speech. The platform creates 5 types of audio exercises: listening comprehension, fill in the blanks (audio), multiple choice (audio), true/false (audio), and answer questions (audio). All audio is generated by AI to match the lesson topic and CEFR level.

Listening is often the most challenging skill for ESL students because teachers lack easy access to level-appropriate audio materials. Traditional listening exercises require finding or recording audio, creating transcripts, and writing questions — a process that takes 30-60 minutes per exercise. Edooqoo does all of this in under 60 seconds.

The AI generates natural-sounding speech on any topic, calibrated to the student's CEFR level. For A1-A2 learners, the speech is slower with clear enunciation and simple vocabulary. For B2-C2 learners, it includes natural speed, connected speech, and complex structures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the audio generated by AI or recorded by humans?

All audio is AI-generated. The speech sounds natural and is customized to the lesson topic and CEFR level. No recording equipment or voice actors needed.

Can students replay the audio?

Yes. Students can play, pause, and replay the audio as many times as they need when completing exercises online.

Are listening exercises good for exam preparation?

Yes. IELTS, Cambridge (FCE, CAE, CPE), and TOEFL all include listening sections. Edooqoo's audio exercises mirror these exam formats.

Can I assign audio exercises as homework?

Yes. Students access audio exercises through homework links or the Student Hub portal. They listen and complete exercises on any device with a browser.

Can I combine audio with text exercises?

Absolutely. A typical worksheet might include a listening comprehension exercise followed by vocabulary matching, discussion questions, and gap text — all in one worksheet.
