English Worksheets for Kids — AI-Powered Generator

Teaching English to children requires age-appropriate materials that are fun, visual, and engaging. Edooqoo generates worksheets tailored for young learners (ages 6-12) with simple vocabulary, colorful picture exercises, matching activities, and basic grammar practice at CEFR A1-A2 levels.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create picture-based worksheets for kids?

Yes! Select Picture exercise types (Describe Picture, Multiple Choice Picture, True/False Picture). The AI generates visual exercises with AI-created images appropriate for young learners.

What CEFR level is appropriate for children?

Most children aged 6-10 are at A1, while 10-12 year olds may be A2. Select the appropriate level and the AI adjusts vocabulary complexity and sentence length.
