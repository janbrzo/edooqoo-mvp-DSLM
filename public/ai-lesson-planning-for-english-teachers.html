AI Lesson Planning for English Teachers

Edooqoo uses AI to streamline the entire lesson planning workflow for English teachers. From generating worksheets to suggesting topics based on student progress data, the platform handles material creation so teachers can focus on teaching. Teachers report saving 3-5 hours per week on average.

Traditional lesson planning for English teachers involves finding appropriate texts, creating exercises, preparing answer keys, and adapting materials to different student levels — a process that takes 30-60 minutes per lesson. With Edooqoo, the entire process takes under 2 minutes.

But Edooqoo goes beyond worksheet generation. It's a complete lesson planning ecosystem: AI suggests topics based on skill gaps, the calendar system schedules lessons, homework extends learning between sessions, flashcards reinforce vocabulary, and progress tracking shows what worked and what needs more practice.

AI Lesson Planning Workflow

  1. Review student progress — Check nano-skill mastery metrics to identify skill gaps and areas needing practice
  2. Use AI suggestions — Edooqoo suggests worksheet topics and exercises based on student data
  3. Generate worksheet — Create a personalized worksheet with targeted exercises in under 60 seconds
  4. Teach the lesson — Use Live Session mode for real-time interaction or share the worksheet
  5. Assign homework — Select exercises for additional practice with AI grading
  6. Create flashcards — Add new vocabulary to spaced repetition flashcard sets
  7. Track results — Review homework scores and update progress tracking
  8. Plan next lesson — AI uses updated data to suggest the next focus area

Planning for Different Class Types

1-on-1 Private Tutoring

Maximum personalization. Use Student Knowledge entries extensively — note the student's job, interests, hobbies, and goals. The AI incorporates all of this into exercises. Review progress data before each lesson to identify the most impactful skill to practice. Use Discussion Questions and Describe Picture for speaking practice. Assign personalized homework targeting specific weak areas.

Small Group (2-4 students)

Generate worksheets at the same CEFR level but with different personalization for each student. Use Live Session mode so you can see all students' answers simultaneously. Pair Discussion Questions with peer interaction — students can discuss answers before sharing with the teacher. Assign homework individually to target each student's specific needs.

Larger Class (5+ students)

Focus on a common CEFR level and topic. Use exercise types that work well in groups: Discussion Questions (group debate), Dialogue Practice (pair work), Categorization (team activity), and Matching Halves (collaborative). Live Session mode shows all answers in real-time, making it easy to monitor a large group. Assign the same homework to all students, then use AI grading to save time on correction.

Integrating AI into Your Existing Curriculum

  1. Week 1-2: Supplement — Use Edooqoo to generate additional practice exercises for your existing textbook units. Keep your current curriculum structure but add AI-generated worksheets for extra practice.
  2. Week 3-4: Replace preparation — Instead of searching for materials or creating exercises from scratch, use Edooqoo as your primary material source. Enter the same topics and grammar points from your curriculum.
  3. Week 5-6: Add homework — Start assigning Edooqoo exercises as homework. Let AI handle grading. Use the time you save to provide more detailed feedback on important items.
  4. Week 7+: Full integration — Use AI suggestions based on progress data to inform your curriculum decisions. Let the data guide which skills need more practice. Add flashcards and progress tracking for a complete AI-assisted workflow.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does AI suggest lesson topics?

Edooqoo's AI analyzes the student's nano-skill mastery data — skill gaps, declining trends, and unaddressed CEFR-tagged competencies — to suggest targeted worksheet topics and exercise types.

Can I plan recurring lessons?

Yes. The Lesson Calendar supports weekly recurring bookings with Google Calendar sync. You can plan a series of lessons and generate materials for each one progressively.

How does Edooqoo compare to generic AI tools like ChatGPT?

Unlike ChatGPT, Edooqoo provides structured exercises with answer keys, interactive student completion, automatic grading, progress tracking, homework management, flashcards, and lesson scheduling — all in one platform specifically designed for English teachers.

Can I plan lessons for multiple students?

Yes. Each student has their own profile, progress data, and lesson history. Generate unique worksheets for each student based on their individual needs and CEFR level.

Does lesson planning work for online teaching?

Absolutely. Edooqoo integrates with Google Meet for video calls, supports Live Session mode for real-time interaction, and all materials are accessible online. The Lesson Calendar handles timezone management for international students.

How do I handle last-minute lesson preparation?

Edooqoo generates a complete worksheet in under 60 seconds. Even if you only have 2 minutes before a lesson, you can create personalized materials. Check the AI suggestions for quick topic ideas based on the student's progress data.

Can I reuse lesson plans with different students?

You can duplicate any worksheet and assign it to a different student. However, the power of Edooqoo is that each worksheet is personalized — generating a new worksheet on the same topic for a different student takes only 60 seconds and produces better-adapted content.

How do I track which topics I've already covered with a student?

Every worksheet is saved in the student's history. The progress tracking system shows which skills and grammar points have been practiced. The AI suggestions feature takes this history into account, avoiding repetition and suggesting new areas to practice.
