Key B2 Grammar Topics

  • All conditional types (0, 1, 2, 3) including mixed conditionals
  • Reported speech (all forms including questions and commands)
  • Passive voice (all tenses including perfect and continuous)
  • Advanced relative clauses (defining/non-defining, reduced)
  • Wish/if only for regrets and hypothetical situations
  • Advanced modals (must have, should have, might have been)
  • Phrasal verbs and idiomatic expressions
  • Gerunds vs. infinitives (change of meaning)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is B2 suitable for FCE preparation?

Yes. B2 corresponds to Cambridge First Certificate in English (FCE/B2 First). Edooqoo generates exercises that mirror FCE exam formats including Use of English, reading, and writing tasks.

Can I generate Business English worksheets at B2?

Absolutely. B2 is the primary level for Business English. Specify topics like meetings, negotiations, presentations, email writing, or industry-specific vocabulary.

How does the AI handle B2 complexity?

The AI uses vocabulary from the 5,000-8,000 frequency range, complex sentence structures, and content requiring critical thinking. It also generates longer reading passages and more challenging listening exercises.

Can I track which B2 skills still need work?

Yes. The DSLM tracks B2 nano-skills separately (e.g., "B2.grammar.mixed_conditionals" vs. "B2.grammar.reported_speech.questions") so you see exactly where each student needs focused practice.

What makes B2 worksheets different from B1?

B2 worksheets feature longer texts, more complex grammar structures, abstract topics, idiomatic language, and exercises requiring inference and critical analysis rather than just comprehension.
