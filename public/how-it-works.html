How Edooqoo Works — 8 Steps to Smarter Teaching

Edooqoo is an AI-powered platform that helps English teachers create personalized worksheets, assign homework, track student progress, and manage lessons. Here's how it works:

1 Sign Up for Free

Create your teacher account in 30 seconds. No credit card required. You get 2 free worksheets to explore all 29 exercise types.

  • Google or email signup
  • Instant access to the worksheet generator
  • Full preview before committing

2 Add Your Students

Add students with their name, email, and CEFR level. Edooqoo creates a profile for each student and starts tracking their learning journey.

  • Store student details and notes
  • Set individual CEFR levels (A1-C2)
  • Add knowledge base entries for personalization

3 Send the Welcome Test

Invite students to take Edooqoo's 49-question AI placement test. It covers grammar, vocabulary, reading, listening, and speaking to determine their exact level.

  • Automatic CEFR level assessment
  • Identifies strongest and weakest skills
  • Assigns a learning path: Comfort, Guided, Accelerated, or Target

4 Review the Learning Profile

After the Welcome Test, Edooqoo generates a detailed learning profile with scores across all language skills, confidence levels, and AI-generated recommendations.

  • Skill breakdown with scores
  • Learning style preferences
  • AI summary of student strengths and areas to develop

5 Generate AI Worksheets

Choose a topic, select exercise types, set the CEFR level, and let AI generate a complete worksheet in under 2 minutes. 29 exercise types available.

  • Text, audio, and picture exercises
  • Customizable topic, grammar focus, and difficulty
  • Editable after generation — add, remove, or modify exercises

6 Share with Students

Share worksheets via link or through the Student Hub. Students can access their materials anytime from their personal portal.

  • Unique share links per worksheet
  • Student Hub with all assigned materials
  • Mobile-friendly for learning on the go

7 Assign Homework

Turn any worksheet into a homework assignment. Set deadlines, track completion, and let AI grade student responses automatically.

  • AI grading for all exercise types (including open-ended)
  • Deadline reminders
  • Teacher review and comment system

8 Track Progress

Monitor student mastery across nano-skills. See trends over time, identify skill gaps, and get AI suggestions for what to teach next.

  • Mastery scores per skill (0-100)
  • Visual progress trends
  • AI-generated next worksheet suggestions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to generate a worksheet?

Under 2 minutes. Select your options, click generate, and the AI creates a complete worksheet with exercises, answer keys, and audio (if applicable).

Can I edit worksheets after generation?

Yes. Every exercise is fully editable. Add, remove, or modify questions, change answer options, update instructions — complete control.

Do students need an account?

No. Students access materials via shared links. For the full Student Hub experience (flashcards, homework, lesson booking), they use their email — no password required.

How does AI grading work?

For objective exercises (multiple choice, true/false, matching), grading is instant and automatic. For open-ended exercises (paraphrasing, discussion questions), Edooqoo's AI evaluates accuracy, grammar, vocabulary usage, and provides detailed feedback.

Is Edooqoo free?

You get 2 free worksheets with full access to all 29 exercise types. Paid plans start at $9/month for 15 worksheets plus homework, flashcards, and calendar features.
