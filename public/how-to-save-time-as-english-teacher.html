How to Save Time as an English Teacher with AI

Edooqoo saves English teachers 3-5 hours per week by automating the most time-consuming tasks. AI generates personalized worksheets in under 60 seconds, grades homework automatically, creates flashcards with one click, and tracks student progress without spreadsheets. Here's exactly where you save time.

English teachers spend an estimated 40-60% of their working time on non-teaching tasks: finding materials, creating exercises, grading, tracking progress, and administrative work. For a teacher with 15 students, this means 10-20 hours per week on tasks that AI can handle in minutes.

Edooqoo doesn't just speed up one task — it transforms the entire workflow. Every feature connects: worksheets generate flashcards, homework submissions update progress tracking, progress data suggests next lesson topics. This connected approach compounds the time savings.
