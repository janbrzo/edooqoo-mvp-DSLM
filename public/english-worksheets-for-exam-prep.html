Key Exam Skills

  • Reading: Skimming, scanning, understanding main ideas, inference, vocabulary in context
  • Use of English: Word formation, sentence transformation, open cloze, key word transformation
  • Listening: Note completion, multiple choice, matching speakers
  • Speaking: Expressing opinions, comparing, speculating, discussing abstract topics
  • Writing: Essay structure, formal/informal register, cohesive devices

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create Cambridge Use of English exercises?

Yes. Select "Sentence Transformation" and "Fill in the Blanks" exercise types at B2-C1 level with grammar focus on "Key word transformation." The AI creates exercises in the Cambridge Use of English format.

How do I practice IELTS-style reading?

Select "Reading Comprehension" and "True/False" at B2-C1 level. Add "IELTS academic style" in Additional Info. The AI generates longer texts with IELTS-style questions.

Can I track exam readiness?

Yes. Edooqoo's Student Progress tracking shows mastery across all skill areas. You can identify weak points and focus practice on specific skills before the exam.
