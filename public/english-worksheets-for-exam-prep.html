  • Writing: Essay structure, formal/informal register, cohesive devices

Try It: Exam preparation (Cambridge, IELTS) Task for Adult Learners (B2)

A short task in the style Edooqoo produces, set in a real adult context: adult candidates preparing for work or migration. Use it as is in your next lesson, or generate an editable version tuned to your learner.

  1. Key word transformation: "I haven't seen her for years." (LAST) "It ____ her."
  2. IELTS Task 2 opener: rewrite "In this essay I will talk about" more academically.
  3. Choose the correct word: "The number of applicants has risen ____ (by / of) 15%."
Answer key

1. is years since I last saw
2. This essay will examine...
3. by

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create Cambridge Use of English exercises?

Yes. Select "Sentence Transformation" and "Fill in the Blanks" exercise types at B2-C1 level with grammar focus on "Key word transformation." The AI creates exercises in the Cambridge Use of English format.

How do I practice IELTS-style reading?

Select "Reading Comprehension" and "True/False" at B2-C1 level. Add "IELTS academic style" in Additional Info. The AI generates longer texts with IELTS-style questions.

Can I track exam readiness?
