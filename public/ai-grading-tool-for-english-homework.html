AI Grading Tool for English Homework

Edooqoo uses AI to automatically grade English homework — including open-ended answers like paraphrasing, sentence transformation, discussion questions, and picture descriptions. The AI provides mastery scores (0-100) for each item that teachers can review, adjust, and confirm. This saves 15-30 minutes of manual grading per homework submission.

Grading open-ended English exercises is one of the most time-consuming tasks for teachers. Unlike math where answers are binary (right/wrong), English answers exist on a spectrum of acceptability. A paraphrased sentence might be grammatically correct but miss a nuance, or capture the meaning perfectly with a minor error. Edooqoo's AI evaluates this spectrum.

The system works across all interaction types: worksheet review during lessons, homework submissions, and live sessions. Objective exercises (multiple choice, true/false, matching) are auto-graded instantly. Open-ended exercises receive AI evaluation with detailed mastery scores. Teachers always have the final say.

How AI Grading Works

  1. Student submits answers — Via homework link, Student Hub, or during a live session
  2. Objective exercises auto-graded — Multiple choice, true/false, matching, fill in the blanks checked against answer keys
  3. AI evaluates open-ended answers — Paraphrasing, sentence transformation, discussion questions, descriptions analyzed for accuracy, meaning preservation, grammar, and vocabulary
  4. Mastery scores generated — Each item receives a 0-100 score with AI reasoning
  5. Teacher reviews — Adjust scores, add comments, confirm final evaluation
  6. Progress updated — DSLM automatically updates student skill metrics

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I override AI grades?

Yes. AI grades are always pre-filled suggestions. Teachers can adjust any score, add comments, and confirm the final evaluation. You have complete control over final grades.

How accurate is AI grading?

For objective exercises, grading is 100% accurate. For open-ended exercises, the AI provides reliable initial assessments that match teacher evaluations in the majority of cases. Teachers review and adjust as needed.

Can students see their grades?

Students see their completed exercises with correct answers after submission. The teacher controls when and how feedback is shared. Detailed mastery scores are visible in the teacher's dashboard.

How do homework reminders work?

Set a deadline when assigning homework. Edooqoo sends email notifications to students when homework is assigned. You can also configure reminder emails before the deadline.

Can I add teacher comments per exercise?

Yes. After reviewing AI grades, teachers can add personalized comments for each exercise — explanations, corrections, encouragement, or additional practice suggestions.
