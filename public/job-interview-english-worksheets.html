Best Exercise Types

1. Dialogue Practice

Role-play complete interview scenarios. Students practice both asking and answering questions, building fluency and confidence in interview settings.

2. Discussion Questions

Practice answering common interview questions with teacher feedback. AI evaluation assesses content, grammar, and appropriateness of responses.

3. Vocabulary Matching

Match professional vocabulary with definitions: "deadline," "KPI," "stakeholder," "to delegate," "track record." Essential for understanding job descriptions and interview questions.

4. Reading Comprehension

Read job descriptions, company profiles, or interview tips articles. Practice identifying key information and preparing responses based on company research.

5. Error Correction

Fix common language mistakes in interview answers: informal language, grammar errors, and inappropriate register that could hurt interview performance.

The STAR Method

The STAR method structures behavioral interview answers:

  • Situation — Describe the context: "In my previous role as..."
  • Task — Explain the challenge: "I was responsible for..."
  • Action — Detail what you did: "I decided to..." "I implemented..."
  • Result — Share the outcome: "As a result..." "This led to a 20% increase..."

How do I practice the STAR method?

Select "Discussion Questions" with topic "behavioral interview questions." Add "Focus on STAR method responses" in Additional Info. The AI generates scenario-based questions for students to practice structured answers.

Is this suitable for B1 students?

Yes. At B1, exercises focus on simpler questions ("Tell me about yourself," "What do you do?") with supportive vocabulary exercises. B2-C1 includes more complex behavioral questions and nuanced language.
