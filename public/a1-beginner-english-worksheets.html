A1 is the entry level of the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR). At this level, learners can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases. They can introduce themselves, ask and answer questions about personal details, and interact in a simple way provided the other person talks slowly and clearly.

Creating materials for beginners requires special care — vocabulary must be within the 500 most common English words, grammar is limited to present simple and basic structures, and exercises need clear examples. Edooqoo's AI handles all of this automatically.

Typical A1 Grammar Topics

  • Present simple (to be, have, basic verbs)
  • Articles (a, an, the)
  • Plurals and basic countable/uncountable
  • Basic prepositions (in, on, at, to)
  • Personal pronouns and possessives
  • Can/can't for ability
  • There is/there are
  • Basic question words (what, where, who, how)

Best Exercise Types for A1 Beginners

  • Matching Exercise — Match words to pictures or definitions (low cognitive load)
  • Multiple Choice — Select correct answers (builds confidence with guided options)
  • Fill in the Blanks — Complete simple sentences with given words
  • True/False — Simple comprehension checks
  • Categorization — Sort words into groups (colors, animals, food)
  • Complete Word — Fill missing letters with picture/context clues
