LANGUAGE STYLE GUIDELINES (2/5):
- Use casual, relaxed language with regular contractions and friendly tone
- Include common informal expressions and everyday language
- Keep sentences moderately simple but well-structured
- Examples: "How's it going?", "That sounds really great!", "I'd love to", "Let's hang out", "It's pretty cool"

CEFR LEVEL GUIDELINES (A1/A2):
- Use simple, basic vocabulary and elementary grammatical structures appropriate for beginners
- Focus on everyday topics, common situations, and concrete subjects (family, food, weather, hobbies)
- Keep sentences short and straightforward with present simple, present continuous, and basic past tense
- Avoid complex clauses, abstract concepts, and advanced vocabulary
- Examples: "I like pizza", "She is working now", "We went to the park yesterday"
